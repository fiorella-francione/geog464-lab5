Off-Street Parking across the City of Montreal

One of Montreal's 2030 Climate Plan goals is to plant and maintain 500 000 new trees in an effort to combat urban heat islands. Part of this greening mission involves converting open-air parking lots into green areas.

This map is for borough planners, to identify potential parking area candidates as a first step in the conversion process.

What you can do here: filter between different boroughs under the City of Montreal municipality, then click a clustered parking location to see how many source points it combines, an example street and hourly rate, and its average heat temperature.

How locations are grouped

Parking points within 30 metres are grouped using DBSCAN, separately by borough. Each marker represents one cluster/ parking lot.

How to interpret the rankings

Rankings use satellite surface temperatures from one day, 21 August 2025. These are candidates to investigate — not recommendations or a determination that a lot should be converted.
